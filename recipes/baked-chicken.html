Baked Chicken



Blackened chicken is quick and easy to make at home. Adjust the spices in this recipe to suit your taste. This is great on a summer's day with fresh corn and mashed potatoes.


Ingrediants

  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cumin
  • ¼ teaspoon dried thyme
  • ⅛ teaspoon onion powder
  • ⅛ teaspoon ground white pepper
  • ⅛ teaspoon sal
  • 2 skinless, boneless chicken
  • cooking spray

Steps

  1. Step 1 :

    2. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Spray a baking sheet with cooking spray.

  2. Step 2 :

    3. Heat a cast iron skillet over high heat until it is smoking hot, about 3 to 5 minutes.

  3. Step 3 :

    4. While the skillet is heating, mix paprika, cayenne, cumin, thyme, onion powder, white pepper, and salt together in a small bowl.

  4. Step 4 :

    5. Spray chicken breasts on both sides with cooking spray, then coat evenly with spice mixture.

  5. Step 5 :

    6. Place chicken in the hot pan and cook for 1 minute. Turn, and cook 1 minute on other side. Transfer chicken to the prepared baking sheet.

  6. Step 6 :

    7. Bake in the preheated oven until chicken is no longer pink in the center and the juices run clear, about 5 to 10 minutes. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center should read at least 165 degrees F (74 degrees C). A cutting board with two blackened chicken breasts
